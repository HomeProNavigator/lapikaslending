Clark County limits for a first-time buyer

The Nevada Housing Division’s first-time buyer limits, active as of June 15, 2026, for Clark County are a maximum purchase price of $566,354 and a maximum household income of $105,500 for 2 or fewer people, or $121,325 for 3 or more. The Division says some areas inside the county can have a higher income limit. Its site also describes assistance that is not limited to first-time buyers, including up to 5% of the loan value toward down payment or closing costs. Those rules change. This is not a commitment that you qualify.

FHA is a separate program. HUD’s minimum required investment for the maximum insured loan is 3.5% of adjusted value. For FHA case numbers assigned on or after January 1, 2026, the Clark County one-unit limit is $541,287. A lender can require more cash or a higher score than the FHA minimum. Call (702) 460-1077 before you plan around either number.
