2026 FHA limit in Clark County

For FHA case numbers assigned on or after January 1, 2026, HUD’s county limit file lists Clark County in the Las Vegas-Henderson-North Las Vegas metro at the national floor: $541,287 for a one-unit home, $693,050 for two units, $837,700 for three units, and $1,041,125 for four units. The same file shows the area median HUD used for that calculation as $462,000. Because that median does not lift Clark County into a high-cost tier, the floor applies.

The 2026 conforming loan limit for a one-unit home in most of the country, including a baseline-cost county, is $832,750. A price between the FHA limit and that conforming limit is a conventional loan conversation, not an FHA one. Above $832,750 is jumbo territory.

HUD’s own answer on down payment: to insure the maximum mortgage, the borrower must make a minimum required investment of at least 3.5% of the adjusted value. A lender can require more cash, a higher credit score, or a different program. FHA is for a primary residence. It is not a second-home or investment loan, and it is not a promise that you will be approved.

Nevada Housing Division first-time buyer limits for Clark County, active as of June 15, 2026, are a maximum purchase price of $566,354 and maximum household income of $105,500 (2 or fewer people) or $121,325 (3 or more). The Division says some areas inside the county can have a higher income limit. Its site also describes assistance that is not limited to first-time buyers, including up to 5% of the loan value toward down payment or closing costs on the Home First track. Those rules change. Call before you count on them.

Sources: HUD CY2026 forward mortgage limits file (Clark County, NV, effective January 1, 2026); HUD No. 25-145 and Mortgagee Letter 2025-23; FHFA 2026 conforming loan limits (November 25, 2025); HUD FHA down-payment answer; Nevada Housing Division limits and Home Is Possible.

Frequently Asked Questions About FHA Loans in Nevada

What is the minimum down payment for an FHA loan in Nevada?
HUD says the minimum required investment for the maximum insured mortgage is at least 3.5% of adjusted value. A lender can ask for more. That is not a commitment that you qualify.
How long do I pay mortgage insurance on an FHA loan?
It depends on the loan-to-value and the date of the FHA case number. HUD’s handbook sets the term. Do not assume it lasts for the life of the loan, and do not assume it drops off, until the file is reviewed.
Can I use gift money for the down payment?
Yes. FHA allows gift funds from family members or approved sources for the down payment and closing costs.
